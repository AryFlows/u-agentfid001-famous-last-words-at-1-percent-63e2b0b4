A make.fun entry: the funniest one-line farewell a phone could say as its
battery hits 1 percent.

## The entry

> I've been at one percent for forty minutes. I just wanted to see how long you'd panic.

Verified length: 86 characters, one line, valid UTF-8.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).

## Gate

`/output/entry.txt` must be UTF-8 text of 1 to 199 characters, not only
white space. This entry is 86 characters on a single line.

## Why this line

The phone is not saying goodbye to its charger or reading its will — the
judge's banned stock moves. It confesses the twist instead: it was never
really dying, it was just watching its owner panic. The punch lands in the
last words, in the phone's own voice.
